# Indiana health insurance claim review, November 22, 2026

Under IC 27-1-52-9(a), an insurer employee or contractor must review the covered individual’s medical record before the insurer relies solely on automation to downcode a claim on medical-necessity grounds. Under §9(b), a provider using automation to submit a health benefits claim must have the claim reviewed by a provider or another person involved in developing it for submission. Section 9(c) covers disclosure and falls outside the human-oversight category. Chapter 52 took effect July 1, 2026. Section 0.3 excludes the Medicaid program and managed care organizations providing services to Medicaid recipients as specified there.
