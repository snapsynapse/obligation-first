- Graph validation rejects exact-day decisions before their own proceeding's filing, vacating decisions before the target decision, and future verified/retrieved dates. Same-day events have no invented clock order. Missing, partial or invalid dates do not establish chronology; schema validation still rejects invalid dates. Retrospective commencement is not prohibited by a blanket parent-date rule.

Exact IRI sets and explicit evidence boundaries are checked. These journeys do not infer legal applicability or confer operative force on a draft.

Synthetic controls exercise explicit exclusions and derivative-date comparisons without banning retrospective terms. See `reference/term-boundary-checks.md`.
- Removal or retargeting of existing relationships requires the exact old/new sets, review reason/time and an accepted owner source-admission reference. The comparison reads the committed baseline independently of the editable fingerprint; a baseline rewrite alone cannot approve a migration. See [relationship migration review](relationship-migration-review.md) for the bounded coverage and evidence contract.

`canonical_source_conflicted` in AI Incident Law describes the canonical material contradicted by the incident. It is not a Boolean source-review-conflict flag. `source_review_unresolved` preserves actual outstanding review questions. Unknown current docket status is not mislabeled as an established conflict, and absent evidence remains unknown.
